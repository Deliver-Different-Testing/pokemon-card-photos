# eBay business policies to create (Seller Hub > Account > Business policies)

Item location on every listing: Denver, Colorado, United States.
Handling time on every policy: 1 business day.
Domestic (US) only to start.

## Shipping policy 1 — name exactly: Cards - Standard Envelope
- Use: raw cards worth under $20.
- Service 1 (default): eBay Standard Envelope, flat $5.00. Label costs about $0.70 to $1.20; the rest is packing time. Standard Envelope carries basic delivery tracking.
- Service 2 (buyer upgrade): USPS Ground Advantage, flat $8.00. Full tracking plus $100 cover. Tick "buyer can choose" so both show at checkout.
- Packing: penny sleeve + toploader + Ding Defend protector, plain paper envelope for Standard Envelope; bubble mailer if the buyer picks Ground Advantage.
- Check in Seller Hub whether eBay caps the charge on Standard Envelope listings; if it refuses $5.00, set the cap and make Ground Advantage the default instead.

## Shipping policy 2 — name exactly: Cards - Tracked Box
- Use: every slab, and raw cards $20 and up.
- Service 1 (default): USPS Ground Advantage, flat $7.00 (label about $4.50 to $6.50 at 4 to 8 oz, plus packing time). Full tracking, $100 cover included on eBay labels.
- Service 2 (buyer upgrade): USPS Priority Mail, flat $12.00. Faster, $100 cover, signature available.
- Packing: slab in a slab sleeve, inside the 3D-printed rigid box, inside a Ding Defend bubble mailer. Raw cards: toploader + protector + bubble mailer.
- At label time: add insurance to the sale price on anything over $100; add signature confirmation at $250 and up (charge it to yourself; it is cheap).
- Combined shipping discounts on: additional items in the same package ship free.

## Shipping policy 3 — not created by you
- eBay applies Authenticity Guarantee automatically to single cards that sell at the threshold ($200 or $250, confirm in Seller Hub).
- When one sells, eBay gives the authenticator's address. Ship there with policy 2 packing and tracking; signature required at $750+.

## Returns policy — name exactly: Cards - No Returns (Dane, 3 Nov: no returns)
- Returns not accepted, domestic or international. Listings carry front/back photos and the cert number so buyers can verify.
- Note: eBay Top Rated Plus fee discount needs 30-day returns; we forgo it. Buyers can still open "not as described" cases under the eBay Money Back Guarantee regardless.
- (Previous policy "Cards - 30 Day Returns" exists in Seller Hub but is unused.)

## Payment policy — name exactly: eBay Managed Payments
- Default managed payments, immediate payment required on Buy It Now.

Principle (Dane, 3 Nov): shipping and packing time are costs the buyer pays, never absorbed. Minimum shipping charge is $5 on everything; buyers who want tracked or faster service pay more for it. Card prices stay at market value.
